## 5. Decisions

- HARMOGEN-R human-labeled coverage is limited; do not treat it as sufficient by itself for a broad software-interview model.
- Use human/teacher evaluations as the primary supervised ground truth; keep LLM evaluations as auxiliary/benchmark data.
- Split answer-intelligence data by student before training so answers from the same student cannot leak across train/validation/test.
- SWE-QA should support software-engineering knowledge/reference learning, not be treated as student-answer grading data.
- Campus Recruitment should remain a separate readiness model dataset and should preserve its source split.
- ML Systems Interview Bench and Resume Skill Extractor require schema/provenance inspection before incorporation.
